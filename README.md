**Project Title:** Hospital Management System
**Abstract:**
Hospital Emergency-Room Triage System is a software-based system designed to assist hospital staff in prioritizing patients based on the severity and urgency of their medical conditions. In an emergency room, patients arrive with different levels of illness or injury, making proper prioritization essential for providing timely treatment. The proposed system collects basic patient information and vital parameters such as age, temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and reported symptoms.
